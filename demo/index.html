AnchorBoard-SaaS

Enterprise multi-tenant project management platform, a self-hosted alternative to Linear/Asana. Each company gets isolated workspaces with projects, Kanban boards, real teammate assignment, RBAC, and real-time notifications (WebSocket + email) driven by Kafka. One docker compose up deploys 10 services.
